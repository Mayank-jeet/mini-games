Ludo

Game setup

Total players
Human players
Computer level

Roll a 6 to bring a token out. Race clockwise around the board and up your home column; you need the exact number to reach home. Land on an opponent (not on a ★ safe square) to send it back. A 6, a capture or reaching home gives another roll; three 6s in a row lose the turn. First to bring all 4 tokens home wins.
